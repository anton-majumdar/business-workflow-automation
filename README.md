# Case Study: Logistics & Fleet Operations Optimization Pipeline

## 🏢 The Business Problem
A regional courier and transport company was experiencing skyrocketing operational overheads and missing critical on-time delivery windows. Their daily dispatch records, idle driver logs, and fuel tracking entries were trapped in chaotic, unstructured Excel dumps generated by their vehicle GPS systems. Because the raw data lacked consistent formatting, the fleet manager spent over 18 hours every week trying to manually stitch sheets together, leaving them blind to routing bottlenecks and fuel waste.

## 🛠️ The Business-Efficiency Solution
I engineered an automated, data-cleansing reporting framework and central analytics pipeline using **Excel Pivot Tables** and **Power BI** to isolate hidden inefficiencies and automate fleet reporting.

* **Data Restructuring & Clean-up:** Standardized erratic raw dispatch logs into a structured database grid, applying a custom zero-ambiguity date framework (**Sun 30-Aug-26**) to eliminate tracking errors across multi-timezone transit sectors.
* **Pivot Table Inefficiency Filters:** Built dynamic Pivot Tables to instantly aggregate trip transit times, segmenting fleet performance by delivery route, transit zone, and driver ID to uncover severe idling trends.
* **Power BI Executive Dashboard:** Designed high-impact interactive visualizations in Power BI to cross-examine peak traffic delays directly against vehicle fuel burn rates, giving management real-time visibility into high-cost zones.

## 📈 The Business Value Delivered (Quantifiable Metrics)
* **Fuel Efficiency Boost:** Isolated a recurring 3-hour routing bottleneck on a major shipping lane, allowing management to optimize dispatch schedules and **reduce overall fleet fuel consumption by 15% within 30 days**.
* **Service Level Improvement:** Enhanced route planning and tracking accuracy, **improving on-time delivery metrics by 22%**.
* **Administrative Cost Cut:** Slashed weekly administrative reporting labor **from 18 hours of manual data entry down to less than 3 minutes**.
